Flood Safety: Do’s ✅ and Don’ts ❌

A clear guide to staying safe before, during, and after a flood.

✅ Do’s (What to Do)

  • Before an Earthquake

    • Build houses on higher ground; avoid flood-prone areas.
    • Elevate electrical systems like furnace, water heater, and switchboards.
    • Seal basement walls with waterproofing material.
    • Install check valves in drains to prevent backflow.
    • Keep an emergency kit ready (water, food, medicines, flashlight, batteries).
    • Listen to weather updates and follow official instructions.

  • During an Earthquake

    • Move immediately to higher ground if water rises.
    • Stay indoors if safe; move to the upper floor/roof when needed.
    • Turn off electricity, gas, and water before leaving home.
    • Use a stick to check the ground if walking through still water.

  • After an Earthquake

    • Wear boots, gloves, and masks while cleaning.
    • Boil or filter drinking water before use.
    • Disinfect items touched by floodwater.
    • Throw away food, medicines, and water that came in contact with floodwater.
    • Watch for snakes, insects, and loose electrical wires.
    • Help children, elderly, and disabled people reach safety.
    • Report damaged utilities and follow recovery instructions.

❌ Don’ts (What NOT to Do)

  • Before an Earthquake

    • Don’t ignore flood warnings.
    • Don’t store valuables or documents on lower floors.
    • Don’t store chemicals carelessly (they may leak into floodwater).

  • During an Earthquake

    • Don’t walk through moving water (just 6 inches can knock you down).
    • Don’t drive into flooded roads (vehicles can be swept away).
    • Don’t touch electrical appliances if wet or standing in water.
    • Don’t use candles or matches (risk of gas leaks).
    • Don’t rush outside during heavy flooding.
    • Don’t go near drains, culverts, bridges, or power lines.

  • After an Earthquake

    • Don’t re-enter damaged buildings until declared safe.
    • Don’t switch on electricity until checked by professionals.
    • Don’t drink floodwater or eat contaminated food.
    • Don’t ignore wounds, cuts, or infections — get medical help.

⚠️ One Rule to Remember

👉 Stay away from floodwaters, move to higher ground, and protect your health.
