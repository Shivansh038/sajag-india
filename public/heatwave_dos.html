🚦 Heatwave Safety: Dos & Don’ts

A quick guide to staying safe when the temperature rises.

Do's ✅

  • Stay hydrated and cool by drinking plenty of water, even if you don't feel thirsty.
  • Wear **light, loose-fitting, and light-colored clothing** outdoors.
  • Protect your head with a wide-brimmed hat and your eyes with sunglasses.
  • Check on vulnerable people like the **elderly**, **children**, and **pets** frequently.
  • Eat small, light meals and avoid heavy, protein-rich foods that increase metabolic heat.
  • Take **cool showers or baths** to lower your body temperature.
  • Move to the coolest part of your house and close curtains or blinds to block sunlight.

Don'ts ❌

  • **Don't exercise** or do strenuous activity during the hottest parts of the day (10 AM to 4 PM).
  • **Don't ignore** signs of heat-related illness like dizziness, nausea, or a rapid pulse.
  • **Don't leave children or pets in vehicles**, as temperatures can rise to dangerous levels in minutes.
  • **Don't drink alcohol or caffeinated beverages**, as they can contribute to dehydration.
  • **Don't rely on fans alone** when temperatures are extremely high; use air conditioning or seek shelter in a public cooling center.
  • **Don't eat heavy meals** that require a lot of energy to digest.
  • **Don't leave windows open** if the outside air is hotter than the air inside your home.
