🏠 What to Do When Indoors

If you are in a building near the coast, you have a few options.

During a Tsunami

  • If you cannot evacuate, move to the highest floor of the building and stay there.
  • Stay away from windows and doors.
  • Do not go to the beach or coastal areas to watch the tsunami.
  • Remember that a tsunami is a series of waves. Do not move to a lower floor after the first wave, as more may follow.
