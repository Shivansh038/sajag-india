Earthquake Preparedness Guide

Essential steps for home safety and emergency planning.

🏠 Home Safety Measures

  • Repair deep plaster cracks in ceilings and foundations; get expert help for structural issues.
  • Anchor overhead lighting fixtures securely to the ceiling.
  • Follow BIS building codes relevant to your area.
  • Fasten shelves securely to walls.
  • Place large or heavy objects on lower shelves.
  • Store breakables (glass, bottles, china) in low, closed cabinets with latches.
  • Hang pictures, mirrors, or heavy items away from beds, sofas, or seating areas.
  • Brace overhead light and fan fixtures.
  • Repair faulty electrical wiring and leaky gas connections (fire risk).
  • Secure water heaters, LPG cylinders, etc., by strapping to walls or bolting to the floor.
  • Store flammables, pesticides, and chemicals in low, latched cabinets.

🛡️ Identifying Safe Places

Indoors:

  • Under a sturdy table or bed.
  • Against an inside wall.
  • Away from windows, mirrors, glass, or heavy furniture.

Outdoors:

  • In an open area, away from buildings, trees, poles, wires, flyovers, or bridges.

📞 Emergency Preparedness

  • Keep a list of emergency phone numbers (doctors, hospitals, police, fire services, etc.).
  • Educate and train family members on what to do before, during, and after an earthquake.
  • Refer to the PSHA (Probabilistic Seismic Hazard Assessment) table at grid points for hazard information in your area.
