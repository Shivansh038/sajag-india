⏱️ What to Do Before a Tsunami

Early action and awareness can save your life.

Know Your Warnings

  • Recognize natural warnings: a strong earthquake near the coast, a loud roar from the ocean, or the ocean receding unusually far and fast.
  • Know your community’s official warning system.
  • Identify the nearest safe high ground, ideally at least 100 feet above sea level or 2 miles inland.

Prepare an Evacuation Plan

  • Create an evacuation route for you and your family to the designated high ground.
  • Prepare a disaster kit with water, non-perishable food, and essential documents.
  • Practice your evacuation plan so you can move quickly when needed.
