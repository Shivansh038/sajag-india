What to Do When Outdoors During a Flood

A guide to staying safe when away from shelter.

  • 🚶‍♀️ Stay away from floodwaters

    Even shallow, moving water can sweep you away. If you can't see the ground, you don't know if it's safe to walk.

  • ⚡ Avoid electricity hazards

    Stay far from power lines, poles, or anything electrical in contact with water. Water conducts electricity.

  • 🌉 Do not go near bridges, culverts, or drains

    Strong currents can suddenly surge through these areas, posing a significant risk of being swept away.

  • ⛰️ Move to higher ground immediately

    Go to hills, raised areas, or sturdy tall buildings to get out of the flood's path.

  • 🚗 Do not drive through flooded roads

    Cars can be swept away in just a couple of feet of water. If you see floodwater, turn around and find another route.

  • 🪵 Use a stick or pole if walking

    Check the firmness of the ground and for hidden dangers like open drains before taking a step.

  • 🛟 Help others if safe

    If you can, help children, the elderly, and disabled people, but do not put yourself in danger.

  • 🐍 Watch for animals

    Floods often drive snakes, insects, and other animals into dry areas. Be aware of your surroundings.

  • 📞 Call for help if trapped

    Use a whistle, phone, or any other signal to alert rescuers to your location.
