✅ What to Do After a Heatwave

Essential steps for a safe and quick recovery.

💧

Hydrate and Recover

  • Continue drinking water to recover from dehydration.
  • Replenish lost electrolytes and nutrients with appropriate foods or drinks.
🩺

Prioritize Your Health

  • Report any heat-related illness to medical professionals immediately.
  • Avoid overexertion for a day or two to allow your body to fully recover.
🤝

Check on Others

  • Check on vulnerable family members and neighbors to ensure they are safe.
