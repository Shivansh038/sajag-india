🚶‍♂️ What to Do When Outdoors

If a cyclone catches you outside, quick action is vital.

Immediate Actions

  • Move to a safe place immediately. Find a sturdy building or public shelter and get inside as fast as you can.
  • Avoid coastal areas due to the high risk of storm surges and powerful waves.
  • Never seek shelter under trees, as they can be uprooted or broken by strong winds.

If You're in a Vehicle

  • Pull over safely and get to a sturdy building.
  • Do not drive through flooded areas, as the water depth may be deceptive.
