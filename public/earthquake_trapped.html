🧱 Trapped Under Debris? Here's What to Do

Stay calm and follow these steps to increase your chances of being found.

🧘 Immediate Actions

  • Stay calm. Panicking uses up energy and can make things worse.
  • Do not light a match or lighter. There may be gas leaks nearby.
  • Do not move too much to avoid stirring up dust.
  • Cover your mouth with a cloth or your sleeve to avoid inhaling dust.

📢 Signal for Help

  • Make noise by tapping on a pipe or wall so rescuers can hear you. Tap in a recognizable pattern, like three times in a row.
  • If you have one, use a whistle to signal for help. The sound travels far and uses less energy than shouting.
  • Shout only as a last resort. Shouting can cause you to inhale dangerous amounts of dust.
