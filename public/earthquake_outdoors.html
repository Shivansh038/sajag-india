Staying Safe During an Earthquake

A guide for specific situations.

🌳 If You Are Outdoors

  • Stay where you are, but move away from buildings, trees, streetlights, and wires.
  • If you are already in an open space, remain there until the shaking ends.
  • Avoid standing near building exits or walls, since falling glass, bricks, or objects are dangerous.

🚗 If You Are in a Vehicle

  • Stop safely as soon as possible and stay inside the vehicle.
  • Do not stop under trees, bridges, wires, or tall buildings.
  • When the shaking ends, drive carefully and avoid damaged roads, bridges, or ramps.

🧱 If You Are Trapped Under Debris

  • Stay calm.
  • Do not light a match.
  • Do not move too much, to avoid stirring dust.
  • Cover your mouth with a cloth or sleeve.
  • Make noise by tapping on a pipe or wall so rescuers can hear you. Use a whistle if you have one.
  • Shout only as a last resort, since shouting can make you breathe in dust.
