🚦 Fire Safety: Do's & Don'ts

Simple rules to follow for fire safety at all times.

✅ What to DO

  • Install smoke alarms on every floor.
  • Test your smoke alarms monthly.
  • Have a fire escape plan and practice it.
  • Keep a fire extinguisher handy and know how to use it.
  • Get out and stay out during a fire.
  • Crawl low under smoke.
  • Call the fire department immediately after getting to a safe place.
  • Close doors behind you to slow the fire.

❌ What NOT to Do

  • Don't block exits with furniture or other items.
  • Don't leave candles or cooking unattended.
  • Don't re-enter a burning building for any reason.
  • Don't hide in closets or under beds.
  • Don't open a door that feels hot.
  • Don't hesitate to call for help.
