🩹 What to Do After a Tsunami

The danger from a tsunami does not end when the waves stop.

Prioritize Safety

  • Stay away from the coast until authorities have given the official all-clear.
  • Listen to official alerts for information on safe zones and recovery efforts.
  • Be aware of hazards like downed power lines, contaminated water, and structural damage.

Begin Recovery

  • Do not re-enter damaged buildings until they have been inspected and deemed safe.
  • Help neighbors and family, but do not put yourself at risk.
  • Avoid contaminated floodwaters, which can contain germs and chemicals.
