What to Do During an Earthquake

A clear, calm, and student-friendly version of instructions.

✅ General Safety Actions

  • **Drop, Cover, Hold On:** Get down on the ground, take cover under a sturdy desk, table, or bed, and hold on until the shaking stops.
  • If no shelter is nearby, protect your head and neck with your arms and crouch in the corner of a room.
  • Stay away from windows, glass, outside walls, and heavy objects that might fall.
  • If you are already in bed, stay there. Cover your head with a pillow for protection. Only move if something heavy above you could fall.
  • Use a doorway for cover only if it is very close and strongly built.
  • Stay indoors until the shaking completely stops and it is safe to go out.
  • Remember: most injuries happen when people rush to move during shaking. Stay where you are and protect yourself.
  • Be prepared for things like power cuts, fire alarms, or sprinklers being triggered.
