🔬 The Science Behind Heatwaves

Understanding what heatwaves are and how they happen.

1. What is a Heatwave? ☀️🔥

  • A heatwave is a prolonged period of excessively high temperatures, often combined with high humidity.

2. How They Happen

  • They occur due to stagnant **high-pressure systems** that act like a lid, trapping hot air over a region.
  • This prevents cooler air from moving in and pushes warmer air from surrounding areas into the high-pressure zone.

3. Health Impacts & Urban Effects 🥵

  • Heatwaves can cause serious health issues such as dehydration, heatstroke, fatigue, and even death.
  • Urban areas often experience **“heat islands”** where concrete, asphalt, and buildings absorb and retain more heat than natural landscapes.

4. The Role of Climate Change

  • Climate change has made heatwaves more frequent and severe globally.

Stay informed and prepared during periods of extreme heat.
