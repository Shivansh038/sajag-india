⏳ What to Do Before a Heatwave

How to prepare your home and family to stay safe and cool.

📻

Stay Informed

  • Stay updated with weather alerts and official advisories.
🏠

Prepare Your Home

  • Prepare your home by making sure fans, air conditioning, and water storage are ready for use.
🛒

Stock Up on Essentials

  • Stock up on water, electrolyte drinks, and other hydration essentials.
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Educate Your Family

  • Educate your family about the signs of heat exhaustion and heatstroke.
🗓️

Plan Your Day

  • Plan any necessary outdoor activities for the early morning or evening to avoid peak heat hours.
