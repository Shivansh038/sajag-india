What to Do After a Flood

A guide to staying safe and recovering after floodwaters recede.

🧘 Stay Safe and Calm

  • Keep calm and listen to local authorities for instructions.
  • Stay tuned to radio/TV/official updates for warnings and recovery information.

🚶 Safety Precautions

  • Avoid floodwaters—they may be contaminated or electrically charged.
  • Do not go near fallen power lines or electrical wires.
  • Stay away from damaged roads, bridges, and buildings.

🏠 At Home

  • Return home only when authorities say it’s safe.
  • Do not switch on electricity until your home is declared safe by professionals.
  • Inspect your home carefully for structural damage before entering.
  • Avoid drinking tap water until it has been tested and declared safe. Use boiled or bottled water.

🧹 Cleaning & Health

  • Wear protective clothing (gloves, boots, masks) while cleaning.
  • Disinfect everything that got wet—floodwater carries germs, sewage, and chemicals.
  • Throw away food, bottled water, or medicines that came in contact with floodwater.
  • Prevent mosquito breeding by clearing stagnant water around your home.

🚑 Safety for People

  • Help neighbors, children, elderly, and disabled persons.
  • Give first aid if needed and call medical help for serious injuries.
  • Keep records of damages for insurance claims and assistance.

⚠️ Golden Rule After a Flood

Stay out of floodwater, check your home’s safety before re-entering, and protect your health from contamination and infections.
