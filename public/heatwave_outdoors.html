🚶‍♂️ What to Do Outdoors During a Heatwave

Essential tips to stay cool and safe when you're outside.

💧

Stay Cool & Hydrated

  • Wear light, loose-fitting clothes, a hat, and sunglasses.
  • Carry a water bottle and drink frequently, even if you don't feel thirsty.
🏃

Mind Your Activity

  • Avoid strenuous activity during peak heat hours (10 AM–4 PM).
  • Take frequent breaks in shaded areas to rest and cool down.
☀️

Protect Your Skin

  • Use sunscreen to prevent sunburn, which can increase your risk of heatstroke.
