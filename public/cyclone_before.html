🏡 What to Do Before a Cyclone

Being prepared is your best defense against a cyclone's power.

Prepare Your Home

  • Secure your property by boarding up windows, clearing drains, and tying down or bringing inside any loose outdoor items.
  • Trim trees and shrubs to reduce the risk of falling branches.
  • If you have a boat, secure it or move it to a safe location.

Gather Supplies

  • Create a disaster kit with at least three days' supply of water, non-perishable food, batteries, a flashlight, and essential medicines.
  • Ensure your car's fuel tank is full.
  • Have cash on hand in case ATMs are not working.

Plan Your Safety

  • Know your evacuation route and the location of the nearest public shelter.
  • Stay informed by listening to official weather forecasts and alerts on a battery-operated radio.
  • Make a communication plan with your family.
