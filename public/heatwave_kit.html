🎒 Emergency Kit for Heatwaves

Essential items to help you stay prepared and safe.

💧

Hydration

  • **Water bottles** and **electrolyte drinks** to prevent dehydration.
☀️

Sun Protection

  • **Hats**, **sunglasses**, and **sunscreen** to protect from UV rays.
  • **Aloe vera** or **cooling gel** for sunburn relief.
🧊

Cooling & Comfort

  • **Ice packs** or **wet towels** to cool down quickly.
  • **Light snacks** and fruits to maintain energy.
  • **Extra clothing** that is lightweight and loose-fitting.
💊

Emergency Essentials

  • **Phone** and a **power bank** to stay in touch.
  • **Medicines** for chronic illnesses.
