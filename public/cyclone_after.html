🩹 What to Do After a Cyclone

The danger isn’t over when the storm passes.

Prioritize Safety

  • Wait for the all-clear from authorities before leaving your shelter.
  • Be aware of hazards like fallen power lines, sharp debris, and contaminated floodwaters.
  • If you encounter standing water, assume it is electrically charged and stay away.

Begin Recovery

  • Listen to official updates on recovery and relief efforts on a battery-powered radio.
  • Check on neighbors and family, especially those who are elderly or have mobility issues.
  • Avoid damaged roads, bridges, and buildings.
