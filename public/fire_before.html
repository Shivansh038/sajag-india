🏠 What to Do Before a Fire

Preparation is key to preventing fires and staying safe.

Install and Maintain Alarms

  • Install smoke alarms on every floor, inside each bedroom, and outside each sleeping area.
  • Test alarms at least once a month by pressing the test button.
  • Replace batteries twice a year, or use alarms with 10-year sealed batteries.
  • Make sure everyone in your home knows the sound of the smoke alarm.

Plan and Practice an Escape

  • Create a fire escape plan for your family. Identify two ways out of every room.
  • Choose a safe meeting place outside, like a neighbor's house or a tree, where everyone can gather.
  • Practice your escape plan at least twice a year.
  • Teach children how to crawl low under smoke.

Fire Prevention

  • Never leave cooking unattended.
  • Keep a fire extinguisher in your home and know how to use it (remember P.A.S.S.: Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep).
  • Store flammable materials and liquids away from heat sources.
  • Check electrical cords for damage and avoid overloading power outlets.
