What to Do Indoors During a Heatwave

Essential tips to stay cool and safe inside your home.

🧊

Find Your Cool Spot

  • Stay in the coolest, most shaded rooms in your home.
  • Close curtains or blinds to block direct sunlight.
💧

Hydrate and Eat Lightly

  • Drink plenty of water frequently, even if you don't feel thirsty.
  • Avoid heavy meals; eat light, water-rich foods like fruits and vegetables.
🌬️

Maintain Airflow

  • Use fans or air conditioning to circulate air and maintain a comfortable temperature.
🚫

Limit Physical Activity

  • Limit strenuous physical activity indoors, as this generates more body heat.
