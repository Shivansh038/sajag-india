🏠 What to Do When Indoors

Your home is your primary shelter during a cyclone.

During the Storm

  • Stay in the safest room of the house. This is usually an interior room or a basement, away from all windows and glass.
  • Turn off all utilities, including gas, water, and electricity, if instructed by authorities.
  • Stay low to the ground to protect against flying debris and potential collapses.
  • If the eye of the storm passes over you and the wind stops, remember this is only temporary. The winds will resume just as fiercely from the opposite direction.

If You Are in a Public Shelter

  • Follow the instructions of shelter staff and emergency workers.
  • Stay calm and help others if you can.
  • Do not leave the shelter until the all-clear is given.
