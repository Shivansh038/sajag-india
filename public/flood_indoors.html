Staying Safe Indoors During a Flood

A guide on what to do and what to avoid.

✅ What To Do Indoors During a Flood

  • Stay inside unless told to evacuate by authorities.
  • Move to higher floors or the roof if water starts rising.
  • Turn off electricity and gas at the main switch/valve to prevent shocks or fire.
  • Unplug electrical appliances and keep them above flood level.
  • Keep an emergency kit (torch, radio, medicines, water, food, important documents) with you.
  • Avoid floodwater inside your home – it may be contaminated with sewage, chemicals, or carry snakes/insects.
  • Stay away from electrical wires, outlets, and switches if water is around.
  • Listen to radio/TV/official alerts for updates.

If told to evacuate:

  • Do it quickly but safely.
  • Carry essentials in a waterproof bag.

⚠️ Don’ts Indoors

  • Don’t touch electrical appliances with wet hands.
  • Don’t drink tap water unless it’s boiled or treated.
  • Don’t try to walk through moving floodwater indoors.
