Earthquake Do's and Don'ts

A quick guide to staying safe before, during, and after an earthquake.

✅ Do's (What to Do)

Before an Earthquake

  • Repair deep cracks in ceilings and foundations.
  • Anchor overhead lighting fixtures and brace fans.
  • Fasten shelves securely to walls; place heavy objects on lower shelves.
  • Store breakables in low, closed cabinets with latches.
  • Hang pictures and mirrors away from beds and seating areas.
  • Secure water heaters, LPG cylinders, and flammable products.
  • Identify safe indoor and outdoor places.
  • Keep emergency phone numbers handy.
  • Educate family members on earthquake safety.

During an Earthquake

  • **If Indoors:** Drop, Cover, Hold On; protect your head and neck.
  • Stay away from windows, glass, and heavy furniture.
  • Stay in bed if you are there; cover your head with a pillow.
  • Use a doorway only if nearby and structurally strong.
  • Stay indoors until shaking stops.
  • **If Outdoors:** Move away from buildings, trees, poles, wires, and bridges.
  • **If in a Vehicle:** Stop safely and stay inside. Avoid stopping under overpasses.
  • **If Trapped:** Stay calm, do not light matches. Cover your mouth to avoid dust. Tap on walls or pipes to signal rescuers; shout only as a last resort.

After an Earthquake

  • Stay calm and listen to official instructions (radio/TV).
  • Be ready for aftershocks.
  • Stay away from beaches and riverbanks.
  • Turn off water, gas, and electricity.
  • Use a torch; avoid switches, matches, and lighters.
  • Try to extinguish small fires; call fire brigade if needed.
  • Do not move seriously injured people unless in danger.
  • Clean up spilled flammables (paint, alcohol, etc.).
  • Inform rescue teams if people are trapped.
  • Avoid loose wires.
  • Drink only safe, filtered water.
  • Leave badly damaged buildings with essential items.

❌ Don'ts (What NOT to Do)

Before an Earthquake

  • Don’t place heavy objects on high shelves.
  • Don’t hang heavy items above beds or seating areas.
  • Don’t ignore structural defects in the building.
  • Don’t store flammables unsafely.

During an Earthquake

  • Don’t rush to move around indoors.
  • Don’t run outside if indoors; you may get hit by falling objects.
  • Don’t use doorways unless close and safe.
  • Don’t touch electric wires or metal objects in contact with them.
  • Don’t light matches or lighters if trapped under debris.
  • Don’t shout unless necessary (dust hazard).

After an Earthquake

  • Don’t enter badly damaged buildings.
  • Don’t drink unfiltered water.
  • Don’t move seriously injured people unnecessarily.
  • Don’t panic or ignore official instructions.
