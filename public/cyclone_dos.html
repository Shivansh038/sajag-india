🚦 Cyclone Safety: Do's & Don'ts

Quick-reference guide to staying safe during a cyclone.

✅ What to DO

  • Secure your home and property.
  • Have an emergency kit ready.
  • Know your evacuation route and destination.
  • Stay inside in a safe room during the storm.
  • Listen to official warnings and instructions.

❌ What NOT to Do

  • Don't ignore official warnings or evacuation orders.
  • Don't use electrical appliances if you are wet.
  • Don't shelter near windows or glass.
  • Don't drive through floodwaters.
  • Don't go outside to "sightsee" after the storm, as hazards still exist.
