🌳 What to Do When Outdoors

Wildfires can be dangerous and spread quickly. Here’s how to stay safe.

Immediate Actions

  • Evacuate immediately if you are in a wildfire zone. Do not wait for an official order.
  • Go to a pre-designated safe place, such as a community center or a public shelter.
  • Stay informed by listening to local radio or emergency broadcasts for updates.
  • If you are trapped in a car, stay inside for protection. If you are on foot, find a clear area like a ditch, a pond, or a large rock formation.

Protect Yourself

  • Cover yourself with a damp cloth to protect against smoke inhalation.
  • If possible, lie low to the ground to avoid heat and smoke.
