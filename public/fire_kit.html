🎒 Your Fire Emergency Kit

Having a well-stocked kit can be a lifesaver. This kit contains essential items to help you and your family in a fire emergency.

🔥 Essential Items

  • Fire Extinguisher: A small, all-purpose extinguisher (rated ABC) that you know how to use.
  • Smoke Mask: A mask or filter to protect against smoke inhalation.
  • Flashlight: A battery-operated torch with extra batteries.
  • First-Aid Kit: A complete kit with bandages, antiseptic wipes, and any personal medications.
  • Whistle: To signal for help if trapped.

🗂️ Important Documents & Contacts

  • Emergency Contacts: A laminated list of emergency numbers for family, doctors, and a fire department.
  • Personal Documents: Copies of IDs, passports, and insurance information in a waterproof bag.

💧 Food & Water

  • Water: Several sealed bottles of water.
  • Non-Perishable Food: High-energy snacks that do not require cooking.
