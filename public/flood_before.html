What to Do Before a Flood

Essential steps for long-term and immediate preparation.

🏡 Long-Term Preparation

  • Avoid building in flood-prone areas unless you elevate and reinforce your home.
  • Elevate the furnace, water heater, and electric panel if they are at risk of flooding.
  • Install check valves in sewer traps to stop floodwater from backing up into drains.
  • Ask community officials if they are building barriers (levers, beams, floodwalls) in your area.
  • Seal basement walls with waterproofing compounds to reduce seepage.

📻 If a Flood is Likely

  • Listen to radio/TV/news for information and alerts.
  • Be aware that flash floods can occur—move immediately to higher ground if there is any risk.
  • Stay alert near streams, drainage channels, and low-lying areas, as they can flood suddenly even without heavy rain.

🧳 If You Must Prepare to Evacuate

  • Secure your home.
  • Bring in outdoor furniture and move essential items to upper floors.
  • Turn off utilities at the main switches/valves if instructed.
  • Disconnect electrical appliances—do not touch them if you are wet or standing in water.

🚶‍♀️ Evacuation Safety Tips

  • Do not walk through moving water—just 6 inches can knock you down.
  • If walking through water is unavoidable, go where it is not moving and use a stick to test the ground.
  • Do not drive into flooded areas—your car can be swept away in only 2 feet of water.
  • If floodwaters rise around your car, abandon it and move to higher ground if safe.

⚠️ Golden Rule: Prepare early, listen to warnings, and move to higher ground when needed.
