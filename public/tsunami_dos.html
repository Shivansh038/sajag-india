🚦 Tsunami Safety: Do's & Don'ts

Quick-reference guide for tsunami preparedness and safety.

✅ What to DO

  • Move to high ground immediately.
  • Heed all official warnings and alerts.
  • Recognize natural signs of a tsunami, like a receding ocean.
  • Wait for the all-clear before returning to the coast.

❌ What NOT to Do

  • Don't ignore natural warnings.
  • Don't stay to watch the wave.
  • Don't assume one wave is the only one.
  • Don't return to the beach until you are told it's safe.
