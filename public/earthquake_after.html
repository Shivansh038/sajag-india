After an Earthquake: A Guide

What to do to stay safe and informed after the shaking stops.

🧘 Stay Calm & Informed

  • Stay calm and switch on the radio or TV for official instructions.
  • Be prepared for aftershocks.

🌊 Stay Safe Outdoors

  • Stay away from beaches and riverbanks (risk of tsunamis or large waves).
  • Avoid damaged structures and do not re-enter badly damaged buildings.

🔌 Handle Utilities Carefully

  • Turn off water, gas, and electricity.
  • Do not smoke, light matches, or use lighters.
  • Do not switch on electrical devices; use a torch instead.

🔥 Fire Safety

  • If a fire starts, try to put it out. If not possible, call the fire brigade.

🚑 Helping People

  • Do not move seriously injured people unless they are in immediate danger.
  • Inform rescue teams if people are trapped or buried.
  • Clean up any spilled flammable materials (alcohol, paint, etc.).

⚡ Avoid Hazards

  • Stay away from loose electric wires.
  • Do not touch metal objects in contact with live wires.

💧 Food & Water Safety

  • Do not drink water from open containers unless checked. Filter it through a sieve, cloth, or proper filter.
  • If your home is badly damaged, leave safely and carry:
    • Water containers
    • Food supplies
    • Essential medicines (including for chronic illnesses like heart disease or diabetes)
