⚡ What to Do When Indoors

Your first priority is to get out safely and quickly.

Immediate Action

  • Get out immediately! Do not stop to collect belongings or call for help from inside.
  • Crawl low under smoke. Smoke rises, so the air is clearer and cooler near the floor.
  • As you leave, feel doors with the back of your hand before opening them. If a door is hot, do not open it. Find an alternative escape route.
  • Close doors behind you as you escape to slow the fire's spread.

Once You're Out

  • Go to your designated outdoor meeting place.
  • Once you are safely outside, call the fire department or emergency services immediately.
  • Do not go back inside a burning building for any reason.
