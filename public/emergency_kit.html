🎒 Disaster Emergency Kit

Be prepared for any situation with a well-stocked kit.

Essential Items

🔦

Battery-operated torch with extra batteries.

📻

Battery-operated radio for official updates.

⚕️

First aid kit and a manual for quick help.

🥫

Emergency food (dry items) and water (packed and sealed).

🕯️

Candles and matches in a waterproof container.

🔪

A sturdy knife for various tasks.

🧪

Chlorine tablets or powdered water purifiers.

🪓

Can opener for preserved foods.

💊

Essential medicines for chronic illnesses.

💳

Cash and credit cards for unexpected needs.

🔗

Thick ropes and cords for securing or tying.

👟

A pair of sturdy shoes for protection and mobility.
