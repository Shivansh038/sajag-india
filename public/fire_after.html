🩹 What to Do After a Fire

Taking the right steps after a fire can help you recover safely.

Safety First

  • Do not re-enter your home until the fire department says it is safe. Structural damage and hidden hot spots can pose a serious risk.
  • Be aware of hazards like weakened floors, walls, and ceilings.
  • Discard any food or medicine that may have been exposed to heat, smoke, or fire.

Beginning Recovery

  • Contact your insurance company as soon as possible to begin the claims process.
  • Reach out to family and friends to let them know you are safe.
  • Secure your property if possible to prevent further loss.
