🚶‍♂️ What to Do When Outdoors

Every second counts. Move quickly to safety.

Immediate Actions

  • Move inland immediately and as fast as you can. Every second counts.
  • Head to high ground. Do not wait for an official warning if you feel a strong earthquake or see natural signs of a tsunami.
  • If you are in a low-lying coastal area and can't get to high ground, your last resort is to climb a sturdy tree or get to the roof of a strong building.
  • If you are caught in the water, try to grab onto something that floats to stay above the water.
