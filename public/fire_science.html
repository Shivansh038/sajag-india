🔬 The Science Behind Fire

Understanding the core elements of fire helps us prevent and stop it.

A fire is a rapid chemical reaction called combustion. To start a fire, you need three things: fuel, heat, and oxygen. These three elements form what scientists call the Fire Triangle. If you remove even one of these elements, the fire cannot begin or will be extinguished.

Types of Fires

Fires are classified into different types based on the kind of material that is burning. This is important because different types of fires require different extinguishing agents. Using the wrong one can make the fire worse!

  • Class A: Fires involving ordinary combustible materials like wood, paper, and cloth.
  • Class B: Fires involving flammable liquids such as gasoline, oil, and grease.
  • Class C: Fires involving energized electrical equipment, like faulty wiring or appliances.
  • Class D: Fires involving combustible metals like magnesium and titanium, often found in industrial settings.
